00:06 — FAQ

Frequently asked

Turnaround time? +

Most short-form edits (Shorts/Reels) ship in 24–48 hours. Long-form YouTube videos typically take 3–7 days depending on length and revisions.

How do I track my application? +

Every submission generates an Application ID. Use the Application Status page to check where it stands — no login required.

Do classes run live? +

Yes — small live cohorts over Discord voice/screen-share, plus recordings for every session.

What if I don't have editing software? +

We'll help you pick free or budget software during onboarding — a powerful PC/laptop is recommended but we can advise on minimum specs.
